About

Hi, I'm Yutong Liu. I'm an Embodied AI Engineer and Robotics Researcher passionate about building intelligent robots that can perceive, learn, and interact with the world.

My work spans humanoid robotics, machine learning, simulation, motion capture, and large language models, with a focus on bridging virtual environments and real-world deployment. I enjoy transforming research ideas into practical robotic systems that empower education, collaboration, and everyday life.

G1 Kendama — Big-Cup Catch

The Unitree G1 swings a kendama ball up and catches it in the big cup, in MuJoCo: open-loop swing-up plus a closed-loop catch, tuned by CEM search with the arm in the loop, holding the ken in a custom 3D-printed fist. 30/30 catches under 3% parameter jitter and up to 5 mm camera noise.
